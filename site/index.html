Who we are

Breaking the silence breaks the cycle.

Child sexual abuse survives on the silence of adults — adults who were harmed and never told, adults who witnessed and didn't know what to say, adults who were taught that naming what happened was harder than carrying it. That silence is how abuse moves from one generation to the next.

Brave Voices believes prevention is the work of grown-ups, not children. When adult survivors speak — even when their voices shake — they change what the next child will face. Their stories become the warning, the permission, and the proof that another adult will listen. That is how the cycle ends.

Understanding the cycle

Abuse persists in silence.

Childhood sexual abuse continues not simply because it happens, but because of silence, secrecy, shame, and fear. Change begins when we move from asking "Why didn't the child tell?" to "What made it so hard for them to tell?"
